|      |                      | Neonatal Care                                                           | message 1     | After birth, the baby needs immediate warmth and care. Babies who are small or born early need regular follow-up visits.<br>Family members should wash hands before touching the baby, avoid harmful traditional practices, and limit visitors to protect the baby from infections.
